# Related Work Refactor

The related work relevant to DIC3 spans multiple strands of research that, taken together, inform the proposed approach yet also reveal unresolved gaps.

## 1. Decision support and predictive analytics

A large body of work focuses on decision support systems that use predictive modeling to estimate outcomes and recommend actions. These systems often achieve strong performance under controlled conditions, but they may struggle when the environment changes rapidly or when data quality varies across sources. DIC3 differs by emphasizing context-aware reasoning and uncertainty management rather than relying on a single predictive model.

## 2. Context-aware and adaptive systems

Research in context-aware computing demonstrates that decision quality can improve when systems model situational factors such as temporal variation, user role, environment, constraints, and task urgency. However, many approaches remain tightly coupled to a specific domain or scenario. DIC3 extends this perspective by treating context as an operational layer that influences data prioritization, recommendation generation, and evaluation.

## 3. Multi-agent and collaborative intelligence

Collaborative multi-agent systems show that distributed reasoning can be effective when tasks are decomposed across specialized modules or actors. This is relevant to DIC3 because decision support often requires combining multiple streams of expertise and responsibilities. The project extends this idea by defining a human-in-the-loop structure in which automated components and decision-makers interact under clear governance and explainability constraints.

## 4. Human-centered AI and explainability

A growing literature on human-centered AI highlights the importance of transparency, trust calibration, and the limits of automation. DIC3 aligns with this perspective by prioritizing explainability and controlled autonomy rather than full automation. The goal is not to replace human judgment, but to augment it with evidence-based reasoning and structured uncertainty communication.

## 5. Critical gap

The literature shows strong advances in each area separately, but fewer systems integrate them into a single operational workflow that supports time-sensitive decision-making under uncertainty. DIC3 addresses this gap by combining data fusion, context modeling, collaborative reasoning, and human oversight in a unified proposal.

## 6. Positioning

Compared with conventional systems, DIC3 is designed to be less brittle, more interpretable, and more aligned with real-world operational needs. It places context, uncertainty, and collaboration at the center of the design, making it a stronger fit for complex environments where decisions matter and explanations matter equally.
