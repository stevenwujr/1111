# Project Abstraction A-F

## A. Aim
To deliver a context-aware decision-support system for complex, uncertain, and time-sensitive environments.

## B. Background
Decision support needs to handle heterogeneous data, evolving context, and competing stakeholder priorities.

## C. Context
Situational conditions and actor roles influence what counts as the right decision at the right time.

## D. Data
The project integrates structured records, temporal streams, human annotations, and external evidence.

## E. Evaluation
The project is assessed through decision quality, confidence calibration, interpretability, and operational usefulness.

## F. Future
The resulting model is intended to be extensible across domains with governance-aware, human-centered adaptation.

## Five core capabilities

1. context-aware evidence synthesis
2. uncertainty-aware recommendation generation
3. collaborative multi-actor reasoning
4. explainable decision support
5. adaptive learning and operational improvement

## Three trade-offs

1. speed vs. depth of reasoning
2. generality vs. domain specificity
3. automation vs. human oversight

## Ten detail points

1. decision state representation
2. heterogeneous data collection
3. signal ranking and prioritization
4. uncertainty estimation
5. scenario generation
6. role-aware collaboration
7. explanation layer
8. review and override mechanisms
9. end-to-end evaluation
10. lifecycle governance and improvement
