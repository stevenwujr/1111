# Detailed design notes

1. Define a decision representation that includes context, uncertainty, constraints, and user roles.
2. Normalize heterogeneous input sources into a common evidence model.
3. Prioritize observations by relevance, recency, and reliability.
4. Estimate uncertainty explicitly rather than suppressing it.
5. Generate candidate recommendations with confidence and consequence estimates.
6. Support multi-role collaboration among stakeholders, systems, and reviewers.
7. Expose justifications and evidence provenance to users.
8. Enable override and escalation policies for risky or ambiguous situations.
9. Benchmark outcomes under both offline and operational constraints.
10. Keep a governance process for monitoring model drift, privacy, and accountability.
