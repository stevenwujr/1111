# Trade-offs

## 1. Accuracy vs. latency
Higher fidelity reasoning may improve outcomes but can delay action in time-sensitive settings.

## 2. Generality vs. domain specificity
A general architecture is easier to reuse, while domain-specific modeling often yields stronger performance.

## 3. Automation vs. human control
More automation can increase speed, but human supervision is essential for accountability, trust, and safety.

## Design implication
The system should support layered automation: fast assistance, explainable recommendations, and human escalation when risk is high.
